Management reports

These reports show clinic-wide takings and staff performance, so they are open to administrators only.

Revenue by treatment
What the clinic earned from each kind of treatment. Counts paid bills only, because money owed is not money earned.
Dentist workload
How many visits each dentist had, how many were completed, and what that work brought in.
Daily schedule
The diary for one day, for printing. Open to all staff.
Unpaid bills
What is still owed to the clinic, oldest first.
