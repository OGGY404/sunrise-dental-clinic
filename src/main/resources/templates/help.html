Help

Step-by-step instructions for using the clinic system. If something here does not match what you see on screen, ask the administrator.

Booking a visit for a patient

  1. Open the booking form

    Click New appointment in the green bar at the top of any screen.

  2. Type the patient's details

    Name, address and contact number are required. Ask for the telephone number carefully: together with the name it is how the system recognises a patient who has been here before, so that all their treatment stays in one record.

  3. Choose the dentist, the treatment, the date and the time

    The clinic is open from 08:00 to 18:00 and appointments start on the half hour. Only dentists who are still practising and treatments on the current price list appear in the lists.

  4. Press Register the appointment

    If a box is wrong it turns red and the reason appears underneath it; correct that box and press the button again. Nothing you have typed is lost.

  5. Write the appointment number on the patient's card

    It looks like APT-20260908-0001. The patient needs it to be found quickly next time.

If the time you want is already taken

The system will say "That dentist is already booked..." and keep you on the form. This is not a mistake you have made; someone else has that slot. Choose another time, or another dentist, and press the button again. Two patients can never be given the same dentist at the same moment, even if two people press Register at exactly the same second.

When the patient arrives, and when they do not

  1. Find the visit

    Click Find appointment and type the number, or open Day schedule and click the appointment in today's list.

  2. After the treatment, press Mark the visit as completed

    A visit has to be completed before it can be billed. That is deliberate: it stops the clinic charging for work it has not done.

  3. If the patient never came, press The patient did not arrive

    This records a no-show. The slot is not given back, because the dentist's time was already spent waiting.

Producing and settling a bill

  1. Open the completed visit and press Produce the bill

    You do not type any amounts. The system works out the treatment cost and the consultation fee from the price list.

  2. Enter a discount only if the clinic has agreed one

    Leave the box empty if there is none. This is the normal case.

  3. Press Print this bill and hand it to the patient

    The menu and the buttons are left off the printed page.

  4. When the patient pays, choose how and press Record the payment

    Cash, card or insurance. A bill can only be paid once. Bills that are still owed are listed under Unpaid bills, oldest first.

Cancelling or moving an appointment

Open the visit, scroll down, and use Move the appointment or Cancel the appointment. A cancellation needs a reason, because it is written into the record and the clinic may need to know later why a slot was given away. Cancelling frees the time for another patient straight away.

Looking up a patient's history

Click Patients and search by telephone number, or by part of the name. Open the patient to see every visit they have ever had, newest first. This is what the old paper system could not do.

Finishing your shift (FR6)

Press Sign out at the right-hand end of the green bar. Always sign out before leaving the desk, so that nobody can use the system under your name. If you walk away and forget, the system signs you out by itself after thirty minutes.
